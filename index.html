Coalition: For the PACT Nigeria

The mark is called “The PACT Nexus”. Its intertwining ovals stand for the civil society organisations and youth networks coming together to deliver the PACT for the Future in Nigeria.

  • Intertwining ovals: partner CSOs and youth networks
  • Orange link: the connection that turns plans into action
  • Rising shape and circle: the vision of the PACT for the Future

The Forge Brookside Limited

A premium fitness-lifestyle brand in Abuja, built as a high-end wellness destination for health-conscious professionals.

  • Concept: a “Fitness meets Lifestyle” hub that pairs a state-of-the-art gym with luxury social and recovery spaces
  • Focus: an upscale setting with advanced equipment, personal training and group fitness classes
  • Identity: a bold F monogram in black, white and signal yellow

Crystal Academy

A football academy that gives young players a nurturing place to build their skills, gain confidence and learn life lessons, backed by high-quality coaching, training and mentorship.

  • Mark: a football rising like a sun, with a shooting star for ambition
  • Applications: kits, jackets, flags and stadium graphics

About

Hi, I’m Isaac.

I’m a graphic designer with 5 years of active experience, and I believe every brand has a story worth telling.

My path from managing high-end facilities to founding One Touch Media taught me that details matter and that great design starts with understanding the client. Whether I’m creating a new logo or leading a full brand revamp, I combine strong visuals with practical results.

I have a record of delivering high-quality work that exceeds expectations, and I keep up with the latest design trends and tools. I also communicate in two languages, which helps me design for international brands as well as local ones.
